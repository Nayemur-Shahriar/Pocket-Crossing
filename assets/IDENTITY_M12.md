# Original M12 vector art

43 original stylized SVGs were authored for Pocket Crossing's fictional Bangladesh-inspired world. They use simple vector shapes, original color patterns and no real company logos, commercial recordings or copied game assets. They follow this project's MIT license in `LICENSE`.

- `vehicles/`: 9 road silhouettes (CNG, cycle rickshaw, local bus, small car, microbus, pickup, delivery van, medium and large cargo trucks).
- `railway/train/`: 4 textures, Riverline and Sunrise engines/coaches.
- `water/boats/`: 5 deck-oriented wooden/passenger/fishing/engine/ferry variants.
- `environment/props/`: 25 small scenery and finish textures.

No fonts are embedded in the SVGs. Runtime signs use existing Godot font support and English/icon captions. Pennants are fictional; no national flag is used as a collectible or disposable object. No optional environment ambience is bundled. Supply your own original/licensed stream through EnvironmentProfile.

See `docs/IDENTITY_M12.md` for assignment, dimensions, theme integration, limitations and device QA. The older art files remain available for legacy Resources.
